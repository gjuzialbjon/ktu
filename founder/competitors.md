# Competitor map: Tirana events platform

Researched 2026-11-12, public sources only, every fact linked. Full table in `competitors.csv`.

## The headline finding: the "incumbents" were misidentified

The three competitors named in the original idea document — Kuadr, Enter Event, Go Wild — are **not discovery platforms and never were**. None of them attempted the product this project proposes, so "why did they stall" has a different answer than the board assumed:

- **[Kuadr](https://kuadr.com/)** is a production house ("Albania's leading production house," since 2016): set design, lighting, sound, crew for big shows (Martin Garrix, Steve Aoki, Alban Skenderaj, EURO 2024 fan zone). It sells tickets **only for its own shows**. It is a potential *customer or partner*, not a competitor — though note a [CNA investigation](https://www.cna.al/english/denoncim/zbulohet-skema-trekendeshi-bashkia-e-tiranes-kuadrcom-arber-veliaj-i469891) alleging ties between Kuadr and Tirana municipality figures (unverified allegations; relevant as market-politics context).
- **[Enter Events](https://enterevents.al/)** ("the first Albanian ticketing platform and event production agency," since 2015) is a promoter/producer with ticketing for its own scene — electronic music, clubs, festivals ("WE, THE DANCERS"). Organizer onboarding is a WhatsApp "book yours" link; no public pricing. Active (events Nov–Dec 2026, e.g. [ZAMNA Tirana](https://enterevents.al/product/zamna-tirana/)).
- **[Go Wild](https://gowild.al)** ("Welcome to Albania's nightlife ambassador!") is a nightlife-only listing site — club nights, electronic festivals, brunches. Active (Unum Festival 2027 already listed). No community events, no general discovery.

They didn't stall at building a general events platform — **they never tried**. Each took the profitable commercial slice (nightlife production/ticketing) and stayed there.

**The free community layer (yoga, chess, book clubs, hikes) is served by nobody.** The closest thing found is individual meetups posted on [allevents.in](https://allevents.in/tirane) (a dinner-for-strangers, an expat gathering, a photowalk) and private WhatsApp groups. ## The ticketing/commercial layer (the revenue side)

Contested by established players:
- **[EasyPay](https://easypay.al/en/easypay-and-destil-hostel-online-tickets-for-blla-blla-blla-concert/)** — payments company, major online ticket seller (example: concert ticket **700 Lek ≈ €7, no online fee**) with a nationwide agent network for cash buyers.
- **[Myticket Albania](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1538055894)** (myticket.al, E-Business Solutions) — e-ticketing + staff ticket-check app for organizers.
- **Enter Events** (above) — ticketing for its own promoted scene.
- **Festivals sell direct**: [Kala's own booking system](https://kala.al/local-tickets) with resident-discount tiers; big promoters (Kuadr) keep ticketing in-house.

## Market size signals (the board's ceiling question)

- **The money for big events is real and spiky:** Kanye West in Tirana (July 2026) sold [10,000+ tickets in the first 2 hours](https://www.balkanweb.com/kanye-west-ne-tirane-10-mije-bileta-te-shitura-ne-2-ore/); large-concert prices have run [€120–330](https://telegrafi.com/sa-blihen-online-biletat-per-evente-ne-shqiperi-dhe-kosove/) — high against local incomes.
- **But the online-ticketing habit is young:** per Eurostat data cited by [Telegrafi](https://telegrafi.com/sa-blihen-online-biletat-per-evente-ne-shqiperi-dhe-kosove/), only **8.6% of individuals in Albania had bought event tickets online** in the three months before the 2025 survey. Most tickets still move through cash/agents (EasyPay's agent network exists for exactly this reason).
- **Typical local event tickets are cheap** (€7 concert example above), so commission revenue per ticket is small; volume or sponsorship has to carry the model.
- **Not findable from public sources:** what promoters spend on promotion, what Enter Events/EasyPay charge organizers (nobody publishes fees), total annual ticketed-event count. These need interviews — `/founder-consumer` run on organizers.

## Complaints (thin — flagged per the rules)

Public review mining was mostly not possible: Even8, Go Wild, Enter Events have no indexed review bodies. The only reviewed product is the government TEA app (222 Play reviews, mixed; recent review calling it dead — consistent with the pattern that institutionally-run catalogs rot). AllEvents' Tirana data quality (wrong cities, duplicate listings, broken dates) is itself the complaint: aggregation without local curation produces garbage. **Theme is thin by the skill's standard (<3 linked reviews per theme) — treat as directional.**

## The gap

**Verified:** a complete, curated, community-first discovery layer for Tirana does not exist. The commercial players all cluster in nightlife/production; the general catalogs are government-run and stale or auto-aggregated and wrong; the one startup in the slot (Even8) is small, thin and apparently unfunded. The "secret" from board round 3 — *everyone fights over the commercial segment while the audience-building segment sits empty* — survives contact with the evidence.

**But the revenue side is harder than the brief assumed:** online ticketing penetration is 8.6%, local tickets are ~€7, nobody publishes organizer fees (pricing is relationship-based), and the big-money events (Kanye, festivals) have their own rails or exclusive promoters, some politically connected. The realistic first paying customers are the **mid-tier**: independent promoters and venues too small for Kuadr/Enter Events but ticketed enough to pay commission.

## The threat

1. **Even8** — already live in the discovery slot; one pivot (community events + completeness) from the same gap. Watch it.
2. **Enter Events or Go Wild widening scope** — they have the organizer relationships and the brand in the scene; adding free community listings is a hire, not a moonshot.
3. **Instagram inertia** — the default that beats everyone: free, installed, where the audience already is. The real competitor is "post it on Instagram," same as before this research.

## What this changes for the plan

- The board's "incumbent autopsy" condition is answered: **no incumbent autopsy exists because no incumbent tried this product.** The graveyard the board feared is actually an empty lot — plus one small live startup (Even8) that must now be tracked.
- The ticketing-commission revenue line needs re-grounding: at ~€7 tickets and 8.6% online penetration, `/founder-cfo` must model realistic volumes; sponsorship/placement may need to carry more of the model than commission early on.
- Next: `/founder-consumer` on organizers (what mid-tier promoters/venues pay today, ticketing-migration willingness) — the one question public sources cannot answer.
