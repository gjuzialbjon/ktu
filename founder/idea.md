# Tirana Events App (working name — no name chosen yet)

Current version: v3 (two-tier model). History: v1 full platform pitch (`board/brief.md`), v2 staged validation (`board/brief-v2.md`), v3 as below (`board/brief-v3.md`).

- What it is: A mobile platform for discovering, creating and joining local events in Tirana — one audience, two tiers. Tier 1, free forever: small community events (yoga by the lake, chess tournaments, book clubs, group hikes) — a tool to connect people, no fees, no commission, ever. Tier 2, the revenue layer: big commercial events (concerts, festivals, parties, business events) pay to reach that audience.
- Who it is for: Attendees (free): young people, students, professionals in Tirana; returning diaspora; tourists. Community organizers (free): individuals, informal groups, clubs. Paying customers: commercial event organizers, promoters and venues who already sell tickets and spend on promotion.
- What it sells, at what price: (1) Ticketing commission — commercial events sell tickets through the platform, paying a small % per ticket (rate not yet set). (2) Sponsored placement — events pay to appear at the top of the feed (price not yet set). Revenue is sold only after the free side has demonstrated reach. Promotion packages and analytics deferred.
- Where and how: Tirana first (500,000+ residents), other Albanian cities later. Staged rollout with stop gates: (1) hand-curated complete weekly feed on Instagram/TikTok/WhatsApp, zero code, gated on verified follower-to-attendee conversion; (2) seed community-event liquidity, founders onboarding organizers by hand; (3) pre-commit commercial organizers and sponsors at real rates, or stop; (4) minimal MVP: browse feed → one-tap join free events → buy tickets to commercial ones; (5) scale commission + sponsorship.
- Budget and constraints: Nothing built, nothing spent; stage 1 costs only founder hours. Three co-founders, all part-time (TeamSystem Tirana/Milan; BMAT Barcelona; JP Morgan London) — full-time commitment undecided. Board round 3 (unanimous FUND IF, avg 4.3/10, see `board.md`) conditions funding on: a named full-time founder in Tirana before stage 2; `/founder-competitors` proving the paying market exists and explaining why Kuadr Events, Enter Event and Go Wild stayed small; numeric stage gates written in advance; hard (cash/rate-card) stage-3 commitments; end-to-end ticket experience and sponsored-placement policy before selling.
