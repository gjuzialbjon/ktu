# Tirana Events App (working name — no name chosen yet)

- What it is: A mobile app (Android + iOS) for creating, discovering and joining local events in Tirana — from informal micro-events (a yoga session by the lake for 8 people, a book club, a group walk) up to larger community events, concerts and business events. Anyone can be a creator, organizer and attendee.
- Who it is for: Two-sided. Attendees: young people, students and professionals in Tirana who are active socially, use smartphones and social media daily, and struggle to find events because information is fragmented across Instagram/Facebook. Also returning diaspora and tourists. Organizers: individuals, informal groups, communities, independent organizers, and small businesses (bars, clubs, restaurants, cultural spaces) who want to promote events beyond their narrow networks.
- What it sells, at what price: Freemium. Free for attendees to discover, RSVP and rate events. Revenue from organizers and businesses paying for extra services: event promotion, increased visibility, and attendance/interest analytics. Secondary: in-app advertising and targeted promotions with local businesses; later, online ticketing with a commission per transaction. Specific package prices NOT yet set — described only as "affordable for the Tirana market".
- Where and how: Tirana first (500,000+ residents), expanding later to other cities. Customers find it via Instagram/TikTok marketing, local influencer collaborations, and partnerships with 10–15 active organizers (bars, clubs, communities). Delivered as a single-codebase mobile app (React Native or Flutter), cloud backend, with real-time notifications, geolocation, search/filter by interest and location, RSVP, and a post-event rating and review system.
- Budget and constraints: No budget figure stated; seeking financing. Team of three, all part-time while employed full-time elsewhere (product/project experience at TeamSystem Tirana/Milan; software engineer at BMAT Barcelona; software engineer / AI engineer ex-Skyscanner, now JP Morgan London). No functional product yet — concept and technical planning stage. MVP estimated 3–4 months after funding; market-ready product 5–6 months. Expected first revenue 6–9 months after launch; break-even projected 12–18 months, requiring ~100–150 active paying clients (organizers/businesses). Known competitors: Instagram/Facebook (where most events live today) and local platforms Kuadr Events, Enter Event, Go Wild (each covering only a small share of events).
