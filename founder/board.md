# Board verdict: Tirana Events App, round 6 (prototype narrowed to Tirana and deepened)

**Vote: 3 FUND IF, unanimous. Average score: 3.7 / 10**, the same as round 5. No member moved. The cut to
Tirana won back what eight cities had cost; adding product again without testing anything in the real world cost
it right back. **New this round: the Monopoly lens will move to PASS in round 7** unless real users, hosts and a
full-time founder are reported.

## What moved, and why the scores did not

- **Credited by all three:** the cut to Tirana only (the first real "no" in the product, says Product); the event
  page built from the attendee's fear ("what will actually happen to me there?"): stats, the weather at the start
  hour, and the A → 1 → 2 → B programme; a faster, quieter first five minutes (about 4 events on the first
  screen, removable filter chips, "never show me" topics, collections only when they hold 3+ events).
- **Why it did not raise a score:** for the sixth round running, nothing has reached a real person: no feed test,
  no interviews, no host, no partner, no price. Offers: "a better demo still leaves the buyer's likelihood score
  where it was." Monopoly: each round adds polish a copycat could match in founder hours, and no users, which
  are the one thing a copycat cannot copy.

## The new idea the board noticed

**Visitors and newcomers as a sharper wedge.** Language tags, "English-friendly" rows and the "Coming alone?
That's fine" collection point at people the WhatsApp/Instagram groups serve badly. Monopoly likes it as a
smaller market inside a small market; Offers calls "events that work in English or Italian" a specific outcome
nobody on the competitor map offers. All three warn it is unproven: the ~17 English-friendly events were tagged
by the founders, not by hosts, and nobody knows how many Tirana events really run in other languages.

## Shared risks (raised by more than one member)

1. **No real-world test, six rounds in** (all three, top risk in every memo). No stage-1 feed, interviews,
   hosts or partners. The panel's #1 reason to attend, seeing people you know going, cannot be shown with demo
   people.
2. **The evening is now promised on screen but owned by nobody** (all three). The programme tells a nervous
   first-timer exactly what will happen; no named host delivers it, and there is no minimum-attendance,
   no-show, safety, reporting or host-vetting rule. Product: "the more detailed the promise on screen, the
   worse it goes when the real evening differs."
3. **Perks and the stamp card are still shipped** (all three). No partner approached, no price, nobody named to
   fund "5 partners → 20% off". Each member asks for it to go behind the same flag as the cities.
4. **The paying side has no evidence, price or guarantee** (Offers, Monopoly). At ~€7 a ticket, 1% commission
   ≈ €0.07, so placement must carry early revenue, and placement is unpriced.
5. **Still no full-time founder in Tirana** (all three, every round since round 2). The work left is people
   work, not code.

## Where the members disagree

- **The visitor wedge.** Monopoly would consider making it *the* first market to own; Offers sees a niche offer
  worth testing; Product sees it as a second audience quietly added before the first loop is proven, and wants
  hosts, not founders, to set event languages.
- **How hard to stop building.** Offers and Monopoly ask for no new features at all until the tests run;
  Product's line is narrower: finish the cut (Perks) and stop adding surfaces that could be empty or wrong.
- **What turns it into a PASS.** Only Monopoly has set a trigger (round 7). Offers and Product hold at FUND IF
  but say a demo can no longer move their score.

## Conditions, merged (the checklist)

Carried and still open (from round 5):

- [ ] **One named founder, full-time and living in Tirana, by a written date.** (All three.)
- [ ] **Run the stage-1 experience test** with real Tirana events and attendees; write targets first:
  attendance, week-two return rate, share who bring a friend, and the stop numbers. (All three.)
- [ ] **15–20 promoter/venue interviews** on spend and willingness to pay, using the prototype as the demo.
  → `/founder-consumer` on organizers. (Offers, Monopoly.)
- [ ] **One revenue line priced and signed before any backend or payments**, with a stop rule;
  `/founder-pricing` → `/founder-cfo` must show it can carry one Tirana salary. (All three.)
- [ ] **10+ partner interviews** covering what partners would pay and who funds the discount, then a stamp-card
  model that does not lose money per redemption. (Offers, Monopoly.)
- [ ] **A guarantee per paying buyer type** the numbers can afford ("no measured foot traffic, no fee").
  → `/founder-cfo`. (Offers.)
- [ ] **Check "tajfa" and the hero line with real Albanian speakers.** (Product.)

New or sharpened in round 6:

- [ ] **Finish the cut: Perks and the stamp card behind the flag**, like the other cities, until one partner
  signs and the discount is funded. (All three.)
- [ ] **No new app features until the real-world tests above report.** (Offers, Monopoly; Product: no new
  surfaces.)
- [ ] **Own the evening before showing a programme for it:** run at least one hand-run hosted event with a
  named host, its cost per host, a minimum-attendance and cancellation rule, a no-show policy, host vetting and
  a reporting flow for meeting strangers. (All three.)
- [ ] **Size the visitor wedge:** how many Tirana events really run in English/Italian/Spanish and who runs
  them (`/founder-competitors`); would visitors use it (`/founder-consumer` on visitors). Hosts set event
  languages, not founders. (All three.)
- [ ] **Report conditions 1–3 of the Monopoly memo by round 7** (real users at a hosted event, hosts measured
  with a safety plan, a full-time founder), or that lens votes PASS.

## The chair's closing read

Six rounds: 3.7 → 4.3 → 4.3 → 4.3 → 3.7 → 3.7. The board's view of the team's craft keeps rising; its view of
the business has not moved since round 4, because every new answer has been software and every open question
is about people. The strongest version the board can see is the one round 5 described, now with a sharper
first audience:

> Tirana only. A complete, honest feed of what is on, and a handful of hosted evenings (a stranger dinner, a run
> club, a newcomers' coffee) run by named hosts, aimed first at the people the WhatsApp groups leave out:
> newcomers, students from elsewhere, and visitors who want something that works in English. Free to attend.
> Money later from the venues those evenings fill, then placement; commission last.

The prototype already shows that version better than anything else the team could bring to a café owner, a
host or a newcomer this week. The next step is not a screen. It is ten hosted evenings in Tirana, counted
honestly, with a founder there to run them.
