A new free app for Tirana: everything happening in the city, in one place, one tap to join.

Small community events — a yoga session by the lake, a chess tournament, a book club, a group hike — are free to join and free to create, always. Anyone can post one.

Big events — concerts, festivals, parties — are also listed, with tickets sold in the app at the organizer's normal price (for example, a concert ticket from around 700 Lek), no extra fee to you.

The feed is complete and curated every week: if it is happening in Tirana, it is here, with real dates, places and a way to see who else is going.

You can rate and review events after you go, so you know what is worth your evening.
