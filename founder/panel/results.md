These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- Seeing the app's events reposted in my friends' Instagram stories with people I actually know marked as going — if the group starts organizing through it, I'd follow them in.
- If my friends started sharing events from the app into Instagram stories or our WhatsApp group and I saw who of them was going, I would join the free hikes and chess stuff through it.
- If a couple of my actual friends were on it and I could see them joining something — or a concert we already wanted went on sale there cheaper or easier than the usual way.
- If my friends were actually on it and I saw their names under an event, like a day trip someone already organized, I would join through it instead of the WhatsApp group.
- Let me reserve in the app and pay cash at the entrance, or pay with Easypay or at a kiosk — the first time has to not need a card.
- If the app had its own Instagram with story posts of the free events that my friends would repost, so I stumble on it the way I stumble on everything else, I would probably show up to a free yoga or book club thing and go from there.
- If I could see that people I actually know are going — or if the events showed up in my Instagram stories so I stumble on them the way I always do — I'd try it for a concert ticket.
- If the app's events showed up inside my Instagram feed anyway — like friends reposting a 'who else is going' story from it — and I saw people I actually know going, I'd install it to grab a ticket once.
- If two or three of my actual friends were already using it and I could see them marked as going to something, I would download it to follow along and probably buy a concert ticket through it eventually.
- If my actual friends were on it and I could see people I know marked as going to something — or if the WhatsApp group I already use started sharing links from it.
- If I could see that someone I already know from my WhatsApp groups is going, or join as part of a small newcomers group so I am not arriving alone, I would try one free event first and maybe buy a ticket later.
- If my hiking or language-exchange group started posting our meetups there and I saw faces I know going to events, I would join through it and eventually buy a ticket there too.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
