# Board brief (v3): Tirana Events — free community layer, commercial revenue layer

This brief stands alone. Everything the founders have stated is here; nothing else is known.

## The idea

A mobile platform for discovering, creating and joining local events in Tirana, Albania — built as **one audience with two tiers**:

- **The free community layer (the soul of the product, free forever).** Small community events — a yoga session by the lake, a chess tournament, a book club, a group hike, a themed meetup. Anyone can create and join them. No fees, no commission, ever. Purpose: connect people, expand social circles in a city whose default social life is cafés, and build the daily-active audience. This segment is served by nobody today (not Instagram/Facebook, not the local listing platforms).
- **The commercial revenue layer.** Big events — concerts, festivals, commercial parties, business events — that want exposure to that audience and already spend money on promotion:
  1. **Ticketing commission:** commercial events sell tickets through the platform and pay a small percentage per ticket sold — a cut of money that already flows.
  2. **Sponsored placement:** events pay to appear at the top of the feed. A proven, easily understood ad model.

The claimed logic: free events create the users; paid events monetize the attention. Revenue is sold only after the free side has real reach — the platform never sells exposure it does not have.

**One-line pitch:** "Everything happening in Tirana, one tap to join — free for the community, and the big events pay to reach it."

## Staged rollout (each stage gates the next)

1. **Curated feed, zero code (weeks 1–6).** Instagram/TikTok + WhatsApp channel: a hand-curated, genuinely complete weekly feed of Tirana events, with the small community events as the unique content. Gate: follower growth and verifiable follower-to-attendee conversion at community events.
2. **Community liquidity first.** Seed and personally support small free events; founders attend, onboard organizers by hand. No revenue is attempted at this stage.
3. **Pre-commit the commercial side.** Before the app is built: signed commitments from ticketed-event organizers (commission terms) and sponsors (placement pricing). Gate: a stated minimum of paying commitments, or stop.
4. **Minimal MVP.** Browse the full feed → join/RSVP free events in one tap → buy tickets to commercial events. Ratings, notifications and the rest come after week-two retention is proven.
5. **Scale revenue.** Commission on tickets plus sponsored placements. Promotion/visibility packages and analytics later, once reach is demonstrable.

## The problem (unchanged facts)

Event information in Tirana is fragmented across Instagram, Facebook and word of mouth; nothing is complete. Café culture dominates; small community activities have no channel to reach strangers with shared interests. Commercial organizers rely on Instagram posts and posters with no structured reach or ticketing integrated with discovery.

## Market

Tirana: 500,000+ residents, large young/professional share, dozens of events weekly, high smartphone and social-media use. Also returning diaspora and tourists. Described as tens of thousands of potential active users; hundreds of organizers; an unknown but nonzero number of commercial events already selling tickets and buying promotion. Expansion to other Albanian cities only after Tirana works.

## Competition

Instagram/Facebook (free, hold the audience, no structure, no ticketing tied to discovery). Local platforms Kuadr Events, Enter Event, Go Wild (partial listings, skew commercial; each covers a small share of events). The free community layer is claimed unserved by all. Why the three local platforms stayed small is unverified — open question for /founder-competitors. The commercial buyers (promoters, venues) are the segment the incumbents already court.

## Business model

Attendees: free, always. Community organizers: free, always. Revenue: (1) ticketing commission from commercial events (rate not yet set); (2) sponsored feed placement (pricing not yet set). Deferred: promotion packages, analytics products. Break-even numbers from earlier drafts are void until real commission rates and sponsorship prices exist — to be re-derived by /founder-cfo.
