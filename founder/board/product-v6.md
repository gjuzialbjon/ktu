# Product lens (round 6)
Score: 4 | Vote: FUND IF
(Unchanged from 4: cutting to Tirana is the first real "no", and the event page is the best craft yet, but Perks is still shipped and none of the experience happens anywhere except on a seeded demo.)

## What is strong

- **A real cut.** The seven other cities and the city picker are gone from the shipped product. The Product lens values this kind of "no" more than any new feature.
- **The event page starts from the moment.** The trust line, the stats card, the weather for the start hour and the A → 1 → 2 → B programme answer the question a nervous first-timer asks: "what will actually happen to me there?" That is designing from the experience backwards. Offsets in place of clock times on hidden ask-to-join events show care in the details.
- **The feed got simpler to use.** About 4 events on the first phone screen instead of ~1.75, filters shown as chips you can remove, "never show me" topics, and collections that only appear once they hold 3+ events. Each makes the first five minutes faster and quieter.
- **Craft beneath the surface holds up.** ~155 tests and a "no layout shift" standard checked on every screen.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)

1. **The experience is still untested in the real world.** Every screen is filled with ~34 seeded events. The panel's #1 condition for attending, "seeing people they know going", cannot be shown with 8 demo people. Komoot-style programmes, weather and turnout look persuasive only while the data is invented. With real data, a thin Tirana feed would make the denser home screen show its gaps faster.
2. **The offline seams are now promised but not owned.** The programme ("seated by lottery → three-course dinner") is a promise about an evening that no named host delivers. No minimum-attendance, no-show, safety or reporting rule exists. The more detailed the promise on screen, the worse it goes when the real evening differs.
3. **Focus is still split.** The Perks tab and stamp card ship with no partner, no price and no one funding the discount. Language tags and "Si parla italiano" quietly add a second audience (visitors) whose numbers nobody knows, and the languages were tagged by the founders, not by hosts. Every surface added before the core loop is proven gives the user one more thing that could be empty or wrong.

## The questions I would ask the founder

1. Say the product in one sentence without "and". Does the Perks tab fit in that sentence? If not, why is it still shipped?
2. Who is the host of the first real dinner shown with a programme, by name, and what happens on screen if only 2 of 6 show up?
3. How many real Tirana events this week actually run in English, Italian or Spanish? (Open question → /founder-competitors; whether visitors would use the app → /founder-consumer.)
4. What does a user see on day one with real data and no friends in the app, given that seeing friends going is the main reason to attend?
5. Why did the event page come before the stage-1 feed test with targets set in advance?
6. What do hosts cost, and how long can three part-timers run this? (Open → /founder-cfo.)

## If FUND IF: the conditions

1. **Finish the cut.** Take Perks and the stamp card out of the shipped app (keep them behind a flag, the way the cities are) until week-two retention is proven.
2. **Run the experience test now** with real Tirana events and real attendees, and write the week-two return target and stop rule before starting.
3. **Own the evening before showing a programme for it:** named hosts, minimum attendance, cancellation and no-show rules, host vetting and a reporting flow for meeting strangers.
4. **One founder full-time in Tirana** by a written date. The work left is people work, not code.
5. **Hosts, not founders, set event languages**, and check "tajfa" and the hero line with real Albanian speakers.
