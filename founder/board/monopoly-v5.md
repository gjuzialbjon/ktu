# Monopoly lens (round 5)
Score: 3 | Vote: FUND IF

Moved from 4 to 3. The prototype adds no monopoly evidence: software was never the moat, and building it shows the copying cost is founder hours. Two things pulled the score down. Scope widened from one city to eight with no research, which is the opposite of "own a small market first". And round 4's gates (full-time founder, retention before organizer build) were skipped. Nothing in the market evidence moved.

## What is strong

- **The empty slot is still real and verified.** No general-discovery platform has tried and failed here. The incumbents only make money from nightlife and commercial events. Even8 is thin. A big share of a small market is still there to take.
- **Platform-run hosted events are the first candidate secret.** Even8, Instagram and WhatsApp groups list events. Running the "dinner with 6 strangers", the newcomers' coffee and the run club with a reliable host is an operation, not a listing. If it works, the know-how (which formats get strangers to show up, and how to recruit and keep hosts) is harder to copy than a feed. It also seeds supply on the free side of the network before organizers arrive.
- **The social graph is built in.** "Your crew", where friends are going, and private events shown to friends answer the panel's top condition (seeing people they know going). That is how a network effect would start, if one starts.
- **Cheap to change course.** No money spent and no backend, so the board can still reset the order of work.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)

1. **Wide before deep: eight cities and three revenue ideas, none proven.** The Monopoly lens needs one market dominated before expanding. v5 adds seven cities with no demand research, plus partner offers and a stamp card with no partner approached and no one named to fund the discount. Commission and placement are still unpriced. Spreading three part-time founders across this guarantees a thin share everywhere, which is Even8's position today.
2. **The market may still not be worth owning, and the moat still sits behind liquidity.** Tickets cost about €7. Only 8.6% of people bought event tickets online in the survey window. Willingness to pay for placement, commission or partnerships is unknown. Until people come back because other people are there, there is no network effect, no brand, no scale economics, and the tech is commodity. A copycat with a WhatsApp group could run the same dinners in month six.
3. **The team keeps choosing building over distribution.** Round 4 asked for a full-time founder in Tirana and proven retention before any organizer-facing build. The answer was more code, including stamp-card and partner features, and no reported feed results, organizer interviews or commitment. The distribution question (how the first 100 users arrive and what each costs) is unanswered after five rounds. Hosted stranger events also add an unsolved safety and trust burden.

## The questions I would ask the founder

- Which one city and one hosted format do you intend to own first, and what attendance and repeat numbers prove you own it?
- Did the curated Instagram/WhatsApp feed run? If not, why was code written first?
- What does a host cost (paid, volunteer or rewarded), how many can you recruit, and what is the no-show rate at a stranger dinner? Open question: /founder-consumer (attendees and would-be hosts), then /founder-cfo.
- Who pays the 20% stamp-card discount, and will one café or bookshop sign? Open question: /founder-competitors (local partner and loyalty programs), then /founder-pricing.
- How do the first 100 attendees arrive and at what cost? Open question: /founder-cfo once /founder-consumer gives conversion inputs.
- Who goes full-time in Tirana, and when?

## If FUND IF: the conditions

Fund founder time only, and no new features, until all of these hold:

1. **Cut to one city and one wedge.** Tirana only. The free community layer plus 2 or 3 platform-run hosted formats. Move the other seven cities, partner offers, the stamp card and the engagement-score roadmap behind a gate.
2. **Put the prototype in front of real people with numbers set in advance.** Run stage 1 plus hosted events in Tirana. Before week 1, write down the attendance, the week-two return rate and the share of attendees who come with a friend, and the numbers that mean stop.
3. **One named founder full-time in Tirana by a dated deadline.** Hosting and partner sales happen in person.
4. **One revenue line is priced on paper before any money is built in.** Either signed placement or commission terms, or signed partner terms with the discount funding agreed. /founder-pricing and /founder-cfo must show it can carry one Tirana salary. If not, PASS.
5. **A minimal safety plan for meeting strangers** (reporting, no-show handling, host vetting) before the first platform-run event.
