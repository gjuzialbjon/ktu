# Board brief (v2): Tirana Events — staged validation plan

This brief stands alone. Everything the founders have stated is here; nothing else is known.

## The idea

A staged path to a local events platform for Tirana, Albania, where each stage is a cheap test that gates the next. The end state is a mobile app where people discover and join local events; the starting point is deliberately not an app.

**One-line pitch:** "Every event in Tirana, one tap to join — starting with the small ones nobody else lists."

## The stages

**Stage 1 — Curated feed, zero code (weeks 1–6).** An Instagram/TikTok account plus a WhatsApp channel publishing a hand-curated, genuinely complete list of every event in Tirana each week. Costs only founder hours. Success gate: a few thousand followers and week-over-week retention. If people won't follow a free, complete feed, the project stops here — the demand validation failed for the price of a spreadsheet.

**Stage 2 — Own one niche.** The wedge is small informal events — yoga by the lake, book clubs, group hikes, themed meetups — the one segment served by nobody today (not Instagram/Facebook, not the local listing platforms Kuadr Events, Enter Event, Go Wild, which focus on larger/commercial events). Big concerts already have promotion channels; the independent yoga teacher has nothing. The founders personally onboard organizers in this niche and attend events to control quality.

**Stage 3 — Pre-sell before building.** At least 10 organizers commit money (a token pre-paid package or a signed letter of intent with a price on it) against a concrete promise, e.g. "30 extra attendees at your event, or it's free." 10 signed → build the app. 0 signed → stop; nothing has been spent on development.

**Stage 4 — Minimal MVP (only if stages 1–3 pass).** One loop: browse the full Tirana feed → RSVP in one tap. Explicitly cut from v1: ratings, real-time notifications, user-created events, image-heavy profiles. Each is added only after week-two retention of the core loop is proven. Single codebase (React Native or Flutter), cloud backend.

**Stage 5 — Monetization where proof is built in.** First revenue lines: ticketing commission (a cut of money that already demonstrably flows) and attendance analytics on seeded events ("X saw it, Y registered, Z showed up"). Visibility/promotion packages are sold only later, once the platform has an audience whose reach is real. Pricing to be set from the stage-3 pre-sales, not guessed.

**Stage 6 — Full-time trigger.** The moment stage 1 proves demand, one named founder goes full-time. The stated reason: a network-effects market in one city is winner-take-all, and a local copycat beats absentee founders.

## The problem (unchanged facts)

Event information in Tirana is fragmented across Instagram, Facebook and word of mouth; no single source is complete. Café culture dominates; small informal activities have no channel at all to reach strangers with shared interests. Organizers beyond the big venues have no structured way to promote or to prove an event was good.

## Market (as the founders describe it)

Tirana: 500,000+ residents, large young/professional share, dozens of events weekly, high smartphone and social media penetration. Also relevant: returning diaspora and tourists. Potential pool described as tens of thousands of active users and hundreds of organizers. No platform covers all events; the three local platforms each cover a small percentage. Expansion to other Albanian cities only after Tirana works.

## Competition

Instagram/Facebook (free, hold the audience, no structure); Kuadr Events, Enter Event, Go Wild (partial listings, skew commercial). The wedge (small informal events) is claimed to be unserved by all of them. Why the three local platforms stayed small is still unverified — flagged as an open question for /founder-competitors.

## Business model

Free for attendees always. Revenue from organizers: ticketing commission and analytics first, promotion packages later. Concrete prices come out of stage-3 pre-sales. The earlier working figure of 100–150 paying clients for break-even is retained as an estimate to be re-derived by /founder-cfo once stage-3 prices exist.

## Current status

Nothing built, nothing spent. Stage 1 can start immediately with zero budget. Informal conversations with Tirana users, organizers and venue owners were positive but are not treated as validation; the stages exist to replace them with evidence.
