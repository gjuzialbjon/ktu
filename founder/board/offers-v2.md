# Offers lens
Score: 4 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong

- **The stage-3 gate is exactly what the Offers lens demands.** Pre-selling to 10 organizers with money attached before a line of code is written converts "would they pay?" from an opinion into a receipt. The build/no-build rule is binary and cheap to run.
- **The guarantee is the right shape.** "30 extra attendees at your event, or it's free" attacks the weakest term of the value equation — perceived likelihood. For a buyer who has never paid for promotion, reversing the risk is the single strongest thing an offer can do, and the founders put it in unprompted.
- **Time and effort score well for the organizer.** Founders onboard personally, attend events, and the result (bodies at the next event) arrives within a week or two. Low effort asked of the buyer, short time to outcome: two of the four value-equation terms are genuinely good.
- **Buyers are easy to find and reach.** One city, organizers already publicly visible on Instagram, founders local-adjacent. Of the four market criteria, reachability is the one this clearly passes.
- **The plan refuses to compete on price** — it competes on completeness ("every event, including the ones nobody lists") and a guarantee, not on being cheaper than Kuadr/Enter Event/Go Wild.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)

1. **The wedge is a market with pain but no money.** The Offers lens says pick the market first, and scores it on pain, purchasing power, reachability and growth. The chosen beachhead — yoga by the lake, book clubs, group hikes — is unserved precisely because these organizers charge little or nothing. Stage 5's first revenue line is "a cut of money that already demonstrably flows," but in the wedge niche almost no money flows: most of these events are free or near-free. The segment with money (commercial events) is the one already served by three platforms plus the venues' own channels. As briefed, the business validates demand in a segment it cannot monetize and plans to monetize a segment it has no wedge into. Open question nobody has the number for: what share of small informal Tirana events are ticketed, and at what average price — /founder-competitors (what the niche actually charges today) and /founder-consumer (what organizers say they'd pay).

2. **The guarantee writes checks the audience can't yet cash.** "30 extra attendees or it's free" is only safe if a few-thousand-follower city feed reliably converts ~30 strangers to a niche yoga class or book club. Followers are not attendees; a 1% conversion on 3,000 followers is 30 people across *all* events that week, not per event. If the guarantee triggers often, stage-5 revenue is zero while founder hours burn; if the founders quietly soften the guarantee, the offer loses the exact term that made it sellable. Nobody has the follower-to-RSVP-to-show-up conversion rate — stage 1 can measure it, but only if the gate is redefined to measure conversions, not followers. /founder-cfo should model the refund rate at which the guarantee bankrupts the offer.

3. **Three local platforms tried this market and stalled, and nobody knows why.** The lens's most common failure is a weak market, and the strongest available evidence about this market is that three incumbents stayed small. If they stalled because Tirana organizers won't pay and Instagram-for-free is good enough, the best offer in the world fails here — "the best offer fails in a bad market." Until that's answered, the market score is a guess dressed as a plan. Open question for /founder-competitors: why Kuadr Events, Enter Event and Go Wild stayed small, and whether any of them ever extracted money from organizers.

## The questions I would ask the founder

- Who exactly is the buyer with money? Name three real Tirana organizers in the wedge niche who charge for their events today, and what they'd pay per month for 30 extra attendees. (/founder-consumer, /founder-pricing)
- What does "a few thousand followers" prove about willingness to pay? Attendees are free users forever; the stage-1 gate validates the audience, not the customer. What stage-1 metric would predict stage-3 conversion?
- What is the honest math behind "30 extra attendees or it's free"? At what feed size, conversion rate and refund rate does that guarantee stop losing money? (/founder-cfo)
- Could you double the price and still be the obvious choice? If the stage-3 package only sells as a token amount, that's a signal the offer is a favor, not a product. What price floor makes a pre-sale count as validation? (/founder-pricing)
- What is in the stack beyond a listing? The lens wants every problem on the organizer's path turned into a stacked solution — attendance proof, no-show reduction, repeat-attendee lists. A feed plus RSVP is a feature, not an offer.
- Why did the three local platforms stall, and what do you know about this market that they didn't? (/founder-competitors)

## If FUND IF: the conditions

1. **Redefine the stage-1 gate around conversion, not followers.** Pass requires demonstrated follower-to-attendee conversion on at least 5 wedge events (e.g. the feed verifiably sent N people to an event), because that number is what prices the stage-3 guarantee.
2. **Stage 3 must be cash, not letters.** All 10 commitments are pre-paid money at a stated price above a token floor (set with /founder-pricing before pre-selling); signed LOIs with a price on them count for zero. A favor-priced pre-sale does not validate an offer.
3. **Before stage 3 opens, answer the two market questions.** /founder-competitors delivers why the three local platforms stalled and what the wedge niche actually charges; /founder-cfo delivers the guarantee's refund-rate break point and re-derives the 100–150-client break-even from real stage-3 prices.
4. **A monetization bridge out of the wedge, in writing.** If the wedge niche proves unticketed, state before building the MVP which adjacent segment pays (ticketed workshops? paid classes? commercial crossover events?) and how the wedge audience is the asset that wins it.

No funds released past stage 2 until conditions 1–3 are met; the MVP build (stage 4) additionally requires condition 4.
