# Product lens (round 5)
Score: 4 | Vote: FUND IF

Moved from 5 to 4. The craft got better and the focus got worse, and to the Product lens focus matters more. Round 4 asked for one thing in Tirana. v5 offers eight cities, private and ask-to-join events, platform-run events with hosts, partner offers, a stamp card and a long roadmap, all built before anyone has used the core feed.

## What is strong

- **Craft is real and goes beneath the surface.** Typed code, ~130 tests, versioned storage, a written copy rule set (inclusive, "never creepy, no pressure"), and themes and timeline colours that show taste. The lens trusts a team that looks after the parts nobody sees.
- **The experience can now be held and judged.** The first five minutes is a demo instead of a description: city feed, today's timeline, "might be your thing", one-tap join. That makes cutting decisions concrete.
- **Platform-run events with a host are the right instinct.** In round 4 I said the offline moment (a stranger walking up to a half-empty event) was the unowned seam. A host is an attempt to own it. Hiding the exact place until a host accepts you is a thoughtful trust detail.
- **The feed-integrity rule exists:** at most 2 labeled sponsored slots per city. That was one of my round-4 conditions.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)

1. **Scope grew in the opposite direction from focus.** Each new surface (Perks tab, stamp card, private events, eight cities, engagement scores, AI event profiles) is another thing to do well with zero real users. The one thing the business must do brilliantly, "show me what's worth going to tonight and get me there", now shares the screen with a loyalty programme no partner has agreed to. Seven of the eight cities have no research and no curator. An empty city feed is the worst first five minutes possible.
2. **The new seams are invisible from inside the app.** Ask-to-join needs a host who replies fast. Platform-run dinners need reliable hosts whose supply, pay and quality are unknown. Partner offers need a till clerk who honours "5% off for the table". Pay-at-the-door QR needs a door person who scans. The demo simulates every one of these handoffs, and these handoffs are exactly where the experience breaks. Safety for meeting strangers isn't designed at all, and the platform now hosts those meetings itself.
3. **Building in place of the experience test.** The prototype went ahead of the gates (stage-1 curated feed, a full-time founder in Tirana). A polished app with seeded data proves taste. It does not prove that anyone opens it on a wet Tuesday. The team still has one part-time founder in the city and has made no full-time decision, so the hand labour that hosts, curation and partners need is still missing.

## The questions I would ask the founder

1. Say the product in one sentence without the word "and". What do you delete from the prototype to make that sentence true?
2. Did the stage-1 Instagram/WhatsApp feed run? If not, why was code easier to start than curation?
3. A newcomer books "dinner with 6 strangers", 2 people show up and the host is late. Walk me through what the app does, minute by minute.
4. Who accepts ask-to-join requests at 11pm, how quickly, and what does the user see while they wait?
5. Why eight cities before one has a retained user?
6. Who is the host for the first ten platform-run events, by name?

Open questions for the pack: whether partners would join and who funds discounts → /founder-competitors and /founder-pricing. Whether strangers show up to platform-run events, and real week-two retention → /founder-consumer, followed by a real-world test. The cost of hosts, and runway with three part-timers → /founder-cfo.

## If FUND IF: the conditions

1. **Cut to Tirana only, with public events, discovery and free join.** Remove the multi-city, Perks, stamp card and partner offers from the shipped product until week-two retention is proven. They can stay in a branch.
2. **Run the experience test the board asked for.** Real Tirana events in the feed and real people attending, with a numeric week-two retention gate and a stop rule set in advance.
3. **Own the offline moment before platform-run events go live:** named hosts, a minimum-attendance and cancellation rule, a no-show policy, and a basic safety/reporting flow for meeting strangers.
4. **One founder full-time and living in Tirana** before any platform-run event or partner is signed. The new scope is even more hands-on than the old one.
5. **Answer the two copy risks with real Albanian speakers** ("tajfa" and the "bad ideas tonight" hero line), because the first sentence a user reads is part of the product.
