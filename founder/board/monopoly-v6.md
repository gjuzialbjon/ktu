# Monopoly lens (round 6)
Score: 3 | Vote: FUND IF

Unchanged from round 5. Cutting to Tirana wins back the point that eight cities cost. A second round of skipped distribution gates takes it away again. The prototype is better software, but none of the four moats (proprietary know-how, network effects, scale, brand) has started.

## What is strong

- **The small market is now the right size.** Tirana only, with other cities behind a flag, is the "dominate a small market first" shape this lens asks for. The discovery slot is still verifiably empty. Even8 is thin, and the incumbents only cover nightlife and commercial events.
- **A possible sharper wedge: visitors and newcomers.** Language tags, "English-friendly" rows and "Coming alone? That's fine" point at a niche the WhatsApp and Instagram groups serve badly. That is a smaller market inside a small market, which is good for this lens. It is also unproven. The ~17 English-friendly events were tagged by the founders, not by hosts.
- **Hosted-event know-how is still the candidate secret.** The programme (A → 1 → 2 → B) shows that the team thinks of the evening as an operation, not a listing. The secret would be knowing which formats get strangers to show up.
- **Scope discipline held on the roadmap.** None of the engagement score, auto-generated events or AI profiles was built.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)

1. **No distribution after six rounds.** The lens counts distribution as half the business, and how the first 100 users arrive, and at what cost, is still unanswered. Nothing has reached a real user: no feed test, no interviews, no host. Each round adds polish that a copycat could match in founder hours, and adds no users, which are the one thing a copycat cannot copy.
2. **The secret is shown, not held.** Showing an evening plan is not the know-how of running one. With no named host, no host cost, no no-show rule and no safety or reporting process, the only part that would be hard to copy does not exist yet. Until it does, a WhatsApp group can copy the dinners in month six.
3. **The market may not pay enough to be worth owning.** Tickets are about €7, only 8.6% of people bought event tickets online in the survey window, and every 1% of commission is worth about €0.07 a ticket. Placement and partner pricing are untested, and Perks is still shipped with no partner approached and nobody named to pay the discount. Owning a market that cannot fund one salary is no monopoly.

## The questions I would ask the founder

- Is the wedge locals' community events or visitors and newcomers? Which one do you plan to own first, and what share would prove it? Open question: /founder-consumer.
- How many events in Tirana really run in English or Italian, and who organizes them? Open question: /founder-competitors.
- How do the first 100 attendees arrive, and what does each one cost? Open question: /founder-cfo, using conversion inputs from /founder-consumer.
- What does a reliable host cost, and what is the no-show rate at a dinner with strangers? Open question: /founder-consumer, then /founder-cfo.
- What will a promoter pay for a labeled slot? Open question: /founder-pricing.
- Why is Perks still shipped? Who goes full-time in Tirana, and by what date?

## If FUND IF: the conditions

Fund founder time only. This lens moves to PASS in round 7 if conditions 1 to 3 are not reported.

1. **No new features until real people use the app.** Run the stage-1 feed and at least one platform-run hosted event in Tirana. Before it starts, write down the target attendance, the week-two return rate, the share who come with a friend, and the numbers that mean stop.
2. **Prove the secret in person.** Recruit named hosts and measure what they cost and the no-show rate. Have a minimal safety plan (reporting, host vetting, a no-show rule) in place before the first event.
3. **One named founder in Tirana, full-time, by a dated deadline.**
4. **Price one revenue line on paper.** Get signed placement or commission terms before any payments are built. /founder-pricing and /founder-cfo must show it can carry one Tirana salary.
5. **Put Perks behind the gate** until one partner signs and the funding of the discount is agreed.
