# Board brief (v4): Tirana Events — two-tier model, now with verified market evidence

This brief stands alone. Everything known is here; nothing else may be assumed. New in v4: the competitive landscape has been researched from public sources (founder/competitors.md, 2026-11-12); its findings are folded in below with sources.

## The idea

A mobile platform for discovering, creating and joining local events in Tirana, Albania — one audience, two tiers:

- **Free community layer (free forever — the soul of the product).** Small community events: a yoga session by the lake, a chess tournament, a book club, a group hike. Anyone can create and join. No fees, no commission, ever. Purpose: connect people in a city whose default social life is cafés, and build the daily-active audience.
- **Commercial revenue layer.** Big events (concerts, festivals, parties, business events) pay to reach that audience: (1) ticketing commission on tickets sold through the platform; (2) sponsored placement at the top of the feed. Revenue is sold only after the free side has demonstrated reach.

**One-line pitch:** "Everything happening in Tirana, one tap to join — free for the community, and the big events pay to reach it."

## Staged rollout (each stage gates the next)

1. **Curated feed, zero code (weeks 1–6):** Instagram/TikTok + WhatsApp channel, hand-curated complete weekly feed of Tirana events. Gate: follower growth and verified follower-to-attendee conversion.
2. **Community liquidity:** seed and personally support free events; founders onboard organizers by hand. No revenue attempted.
3. **Pre-commit the commercial side:** signed commission terms and placement prices from commercial organizers before the app is built. Gate: a stated minimum of hard commitments, or stop.
4. **Minimal MVP:** browse feed → one-tap join free events → buy tickets to commercial events. Everything else deferred until week-two retention is proven.
5. **Scale revenue:** commission + sponsored placement; promotion packages and analytics later.

## Verified competitive landscape (researched 2026-11-12, sources in founder/competitors.md)

**The discovery slot is nearly empty.** The three companies the founders once believed were failed incumbents (Kuadr, Enter Events, Go Wild) never attempted this product: Kuadr is a production house (staging/lighting for Martin Garrix, Steve Aoki shows; tickets only for its own events; subject of unverified press allegations about municipal political ties); Enter Events is a promoter/ticketing agency for the electronic/club scene (since 2015, organizer onboarding via a WhatsApp link, no published fees); Go Wild is a nightlife-only listing site. All three sit profitably in the nightlife/commercial slice. There is no failed general-discovery platform — no negative verdict on the market exists, but also no positive proof.

**Who is actually in the slot:** Even8 (even8.app) — a live Tirana event-discovery app ("Discover the best events near you") with search/save/join and gamification, but thin: ~37 events, "29+ community members," trips/businesses "coming soon," no visible business model. Plus: AllEvents.in's auto-aggregated Tirana page (33 events, badly polluted — duplicate and mislocated listings); the government TEA tourism-events app (222 Play reviews, mixed, a recent review calls it dead); Eventbrite (thin, conference-skewed); Almanart (culture niche, added ticketing 2026). **The free community layer (yoga, chess, book clubs) is served by nobody; small events spread via WhatsApp groups and Instagram pages.**

**The revenue layer is contested and relationship-priced.** EasyPay (payments company) is the major online ticket seller with a nationwide cash-agent network; Myticket Albania offers e-ticketing tools for organizers; Enter Events tickets its own scene; big festivals (Kala, Unum) sell direct with their own systems; big promoters keep ticketing in-house. Nobody publishes organizer fees — pricing is relationship-based.

**Market-size signals:** Demand spikes are real — Kanye West in Tirana (July 2026) sold 10,000+ tickets in the first 2 hours; large-concert prices have reached €120–330. But the habit is young: only 8.6% of individuals in Albania had bought event tickets online in the three months before the 2025 Eurostat survey; most tickets still move through cash/agents. Typical local event tickets are cheap (~700 Lek / €7 concert example), so per-ticket commission is small. Unknown and not publicly findable: promoter promotion spend, incumbent fees, total annual ticketed-event count — these require organizer interviews.

**Implication accepted by the founders:** the realistic first paying customers are mid-tier independent promoters and venues — too small for Kuadr/Enter Events' services, big enough to sell tickets. Sponsorship/placement may need to carry more of the early model than commission.

## The problem

Event information in Tirana is fragmented across Instagram, Facebook and word of mouth; nothing is complete (verified above — the best catalogs are a stale government app and a polluted auto-aggregator). Café culture dominates; small community activities have no channel to reach strangers with shared interests. Mid-tier commercial organizers rely on Instagram posts with no structured reach or integrated ticketing.

## Market

Tirana: 500,000+ residents, large young/professional share, dozens of events weekly, high smartphone/social use. Also returning diaspora and tourists. Expansion to other Albanian cities only after Tirana works.

## Business model

Attendees and community organizers: free, always. Revenue: ticketing commission (rate unset) + sponsored placement (price unset) from commercial events; promotion packages and analytics deferred. All earlier break-even math is void until real rates exist (→ /founder-cfo after /founder-pricing).
